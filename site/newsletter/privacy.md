This note explains what happens to your data when you subscribe to the Malachi newsletter. It is written to meet
the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

## Who is responsible

The newsletter is run by Hector Cardoso, the maintainer of the Malachi open source project (MalachiHQ), who
decides why and how your data is used (the controller). You can reach him at
[contact@malachihq.org](mailto:contact@malachihq.org) for any question or request about your data.

## What the newsletter is for

The newsletter sends the posts of this blog by email, one email per new post. It is used for nothing else: no
advertising, no profiling, and your address is never sold or shared with anyone for their own use.

## What we collect

- Your email address.
- Your acceptance of this note, and the confirmation you give by clicking the link in the confirmation email
  (double opt-in), with the date and time of each.
- The technical data our email provider records with your subscription, such as the language it uses for its
  emails to you.

You do not have to subscribe to read the blog. Every post is on [the blog](index.html), which also offers an
Atom feed that needs no email address.

## The legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a), LGPD article 7, I), which you give by
ticking the box on the form and confirming your address. You can withdraw it at any time, as described below.
Withdrawing it does not affect the emails sent before.

## This blog

The subscription form is plain HTML. The blog's pages, this note included, set no cookie and run no tracking
pixel. Every page runs one script of its own, the color theme switch, written into the page itself: it stores
nothing until you pick a theme, and then only that choice, in your browser's local storage, where the home page
reads it too. When you press Subscribe, your browser sends the form directly to our email provider.

A post's comments are discussions on GitHub, shown by [giscus](https://giscus.app). Its script is the only one a
blog page loads from elsewhere, and only on a post's page: when you scroll to the comments, your browser loads it
from giscus.app, which, like any website, receives your IP address with the request, and which fetches the
discussion from GitHub. The theme switch tells giscus's frame which color theme to show, and nothing else. To
comment you sign in with your GitHub account inside giscus's frame, which keeps your session as a token in its own
browser storage, and your comment is published on GitHub under your account. See
[giscus's privacy policy](https://github.com/giscus/giscus/blob/main/PRIVACY-POLICY.md) and
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The rest of this website, its home page and its documentation, is not part of the newsletter and does run
scripts. The home page shares the blog's theme switch and runs its own animations, and the documentation loads
a diagram library (Mermaid) from the jsDelivr network, which receives your IP address when you open a page of it.

## Our email provider

We use [Mailrelay](https://mailrelay.com), operated by Weblegal S.L., Calle Padre Granda 4, 28806 Alcalá de
Henares (Madrid), Spain, to store the subscriber list and send the emails. Mailrelay processes your data on our
behalf and following our instructions. Its [privacy policy](https://mailrelay.com/en/privacy-policy) states that
its data is stored in data centers in Europe.

Mailrelay can record whether an email is opened and which of its links are clicked. We turn both off: the
newsletter's emails are sent without open tracking and without click tracking, so their links lead straight to
their pages.

## How long we keep your data

- Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
- If you never confirm your address, we delete the unconfirmed subscription within 30 days.

After you unsubscribe, your address stays on the list marked as unsubscribed, so that it is never emailed again.
If you also want it deleted, ask us and we delete it.

## How to unsubscribe

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click removes you from the list. You can also write to
[contact@malachihq.org](mailto:contact@malachihq.org).

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- know whether we hold your data, and get a copy of it;
- correct it;
- have it deleted;
- restrict or object to its processing;
- receive it in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of them, write to [contact@malachihq.org](mailto:contact@malachihq.org). We answer within 15
days. You can also complain to a data protection authority:

- in the European Union, the authority of your country, for example the Spanish
  [AEPD](https://www.aepd.es);
- in Brazil, the [ANPD](https://www.gov.br/anpd).

## Changes to this note

If this note changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. A change in how your data is
used is announced by email before it takes effect.
